Get the newest numbers from Respond.io and 3CX
  1. One time only: drag this button to your Chrome bookmarks bar → WP Refresh
  2. One time only: also drag this one → 3CX Refresh
  3. Open Respond.io Contacts (logged in) and click WP Refresh. Wait about 1 minute until it shows Updated.
  4. Open 3CX (logged in) and click 3CX Refresh. The dashboard opens and saves the call counts by itself.
  5. Come back here and press ↻ Reload.

Each press saves today's numbers. Existing Called for a day = the last update of that day − the last update of the day before, so press it once at the end of each working day.
